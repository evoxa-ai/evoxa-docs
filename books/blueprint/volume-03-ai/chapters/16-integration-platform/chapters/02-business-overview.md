# 02 — Business Overview

> *The Integration Platform enables organizations to eliminate fragmented integrations by providing a centralized, governed and AI-native enterprise integration ecosystem. It accelerates digital transformation, reduces operational costs and creates a scalable foundation for enterprise automation.*

---

# Executive Summary

Modern organizations operate dozens—or even hundreds—of business applications.

These applications often evolve independently, creating disconnected silos that increase operational complexity and reduce organizational agility.

The Integration Platform addresses this challenge by becoming the centralized integration hub that connects every enterprise system through standardized APIs, reusable connectors, event-driven communication and intelligent workflow orchestration.

Instead of building custom integrations for every project, organizations build once and reuse continuously.
