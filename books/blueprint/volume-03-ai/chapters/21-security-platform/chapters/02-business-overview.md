# 02 — Business Overview

> *The Business Overview chapter defines the strategic business context for the EVOXA Security Platform. It explains why enterprise security is a fundamental business capability, identifies organizational drivers, stakeholders, regulatory obligations and business objectives, and establishes how cybersecurity enables sustainable digital transformation rather than acting solely as a defensive function.*

---

# Executive Summary

Cybersecurity has evolved from a technical concern into a strategic business capability.

Organizations increasingly depend on cloud computing, digital services, Artificial Intelligence, APIs, remote workforces and data-driven decision making.

As digital dependence grows, cyber risk becomes business risk.

The EVOXA Security Platform enables organizations to innovate with confidence by integrating security into every business process, technology platform and product lifecycle.

Rather than slowing innovation, security becomes an accelerator for trusted digital transformation.
