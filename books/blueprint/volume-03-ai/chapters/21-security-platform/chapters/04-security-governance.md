# 04 — Security Governance

> *The Security Governance chapter defines the governance framework responsible for directing, controlling and continuously improving cybersecurity across the EVOXA Enterprise Platform. It establishes the organizational structure, governance bodies, policies, decision-making processes, accountability model and risk management framework that ensure security aligns with business strategy and regulatory obligations.*

---

# Executive Summary

Security Governance provides the organizational foundation that transforms cybersecurity from a collection of technical controls into an enterprise business capability.

The EVOXA Security Governance Framework ensures that cybersecurity decisions are:

- Business aligned
- Risk driven
- Policy based
- Measurable
- Auditable
- Continuously improved

Security becomes an executive responsibility shared across the organization rather than being owned solely by the IT department.

---

# Vision

The governance model follows one guiding principle:

> **Cybersecurity Is Governed as a Strategic Business Capability.**

---

# Mission

Provide governance structures, policies, standards and oversight mechanisms that ensure enterprise security remains aligned with business objectives, regulatory requirements and evolving cyber threats.

# Key Takeaways

- Security Governance provides the executive framework that aligns cybersecurity with business objectives, risk management and regulatory compliance.
- Clear governance structures, defined accountability and standardized policies ensure consistent security decisions across the enterprise.
- Continuous measurement, automation and maturity assessments enable governance to evolve alongside technology and business needs.
- This governance model establishes the leadership and oversight foundation for all remaining security capabilities within the EVOXA Security Platform.
