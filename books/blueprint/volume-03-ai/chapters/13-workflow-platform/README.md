## Overview

The Workflow Platform is the enterprise orchestration engine of the EVOXA ecosystem.

It provides a unified platform for designing, executing, monitoring and optimizing business processes through AI-assisted automation, event-driven execution and multi-agent orchestration.

The platform combines Business Process Management (BPM), workflow automation, AI agents, event-driven architecture and low-code development into a single enterprise solution.

Unlike traditional workflow engines, the EVOXA Workflow Platform enables intelligent process execution through autonomous AI agents capable of planning, collaborating and adapting workflows dynamically.

---

## Objectives

The Workflow Platform enables organizations to:

- Design enterprise workflows visually.
- Execute long-running business processes.
- Coordinate AI agents.
- Automate business operations.
- Support Human-in-the-Loop approvals.
- Integrate enterprise applications.
- Execute event-driven workflows.
- Manage workflow versions.
- Analyze business processes.
- Optimize operational efficiency.
