# Volume 3 — Chapter 09
# AI Platform

---

## Overview

The AI Platform is the intelligence layer of the EVOXA ecosystem.

It provides a unified architecture for Large Language Models (LLMs), AI Agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Memory Systems, Prompt Engineering, Knowledge Management, Enterprise Automation and AI Governance.

Unlike a traditional chatbot platform, EVOXA AI Platform is designed as a complete enterprise AI operating layer capable of orchestrating intelligent services across every EVOXA application.
