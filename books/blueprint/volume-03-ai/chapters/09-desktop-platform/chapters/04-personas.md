# Chapter 04 — AI Personas

> *The AI Personas chapter defines the different user archetypes interacting with the EVOXA AI Platform. Each persona represents a specific role, responsibilities, objectives, permissions and interaction model. Understanding these personas ensures that the platform delivers the right AI capabilities to the right users while maintaining governance, security and usability.*

---

# Executive Summary

The EVOXA AI Platform is designed for organizations rather than individual consumers.

Unlike traditional AI assistants that assume every user has the same needs, EVOXA adapts its behavior according to organizational role, permissions, business context and AI maturity.

Each persona has:

- Different goals
- Different permissions
- Different AI capabilities
- Different workflows
- Different governance requirements
- Different interfaces

This role-based approach ensures secure and effective adoption of AI across the enterprise.

---

# Objectives

The AI Personas model shall:

- Define enterprise user types.
- Standardize AI experiences.
- Support role-based interactions.
- Enable permission-aware AI.
- Improve productivity.
- Strengthen governance.
- Personalize AI behavior.

# Key Takeaways

- The EVOXA AI Platform provides role-aware AI experiences tailored to different organizational responsibilities rather than a one-size-fits-all assistant.
