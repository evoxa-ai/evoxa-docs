# 21 — AI Service Business Value & Economics

> *The AI Service Business Value & Economics framework defines how EVOXA transforms AI capabilities, services and investments into measurable business outcomes, economic value and sustainable enterprise growth.*

---

# Executive Summary

AI Services must not be evaluated exclusively through technical metrics.

A technically successful AI Service does not necessarily create business value.

Therefore:
