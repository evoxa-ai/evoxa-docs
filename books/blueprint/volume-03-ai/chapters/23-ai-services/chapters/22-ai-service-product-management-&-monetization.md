# 22 — AI Service Product Management & Monetization

> *AI Service Product Management & Monetization defines how EVOXA transforms AI capabilities into governed, measurable, adoptable, scalable and economically sustainable products and services.*

---

# Executive Summary

AI Services are not only technical capabilities.

They can become:

- Enterprise products
- Customer-facing products
- Internal products
- Platform capabilities
- Commercial services
- Revenue-generating products
- Productivity products
- Decision-support products
- Automation products

Therefore, EVOXA requires a dedicated product management model for AI.

The fundamental transformation is:
