# Chapter 23 — AI Services

## Overview

The AI Services chapter defines the enterprise service layer responsible for exposing Artificial Intelligence capabilities as secure, reusable and governed services across the EVOXA ecosystem.

Rather than allowing applications to communicate directly with foundation models, the AI Services Platform provides standardized APIs, orchestration, governance, observability and lifecycle management for every AI capability.

This chapter documents the enterprise architecture, operational model and implementation guidelines for AI services delivered across cloud, enterprise applications and intelligent agents.

---

## Objectives

- Standardize enterprise AI services
- Expose AI capabilities through APIs
- Provide reusable AI business services
- Support multimodal AI
- Enable secure AI consumption
- Govern AI service lifecycle
- Improve observability
- Reduce AI integration complexity
