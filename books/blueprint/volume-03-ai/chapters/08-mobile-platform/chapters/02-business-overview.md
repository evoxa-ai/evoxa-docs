# Chapter 02 — Business Overview

> *The Business Overview chapter defines the business vision, strategic objectives, market positioning, customer value proposition and commercial model of the EVOXA Mobile Platform. It establishes why the platform exists, who it serves and how it contributes to the EVOXA ecosystem.*

---

# Executive Summary

The EVOXA Mobile Platform enables organizations to bring AI-powered business processes directly to smartphones and tablets.

Modern enterprise users expect secure access to information, workflows and artificial intelligence from anywhere. The Mobile Platform addresses this demand by providing a unified mobile experience connected to the EVOXA ecosystem.

Rather than functioning as an isolated application, the Mobile Platform acts as a secure extension of every EVOXA service, including AI assistants, workflows, APIs, analytics and enterprise operations.

---

# Business Vision

Create the world's most intelligent enterprise mobile platform where every employee, customer and partner can securely access AI-powered business capabilities from any mobile device.

---

# Mission

Deliver secure, scalable and AI-native mobile applications that improve productivity, automate business processes and provide exceptional user experiences across every industry.
