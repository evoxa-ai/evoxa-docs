# 23 — Business Continuity

> *The Business Continuity chapter defines the enterprise framework for ensuring that critical business capabilities remain operational before, during and after disruptive events. It establishes governance, continuity planning, resilience strategies, crisis management, workforce continuity, technology recovery and continuous improvement to guarantee uninterrupted delivery of essential business services across the EVOXA ecosystem.*

---

# Executive Summary

Business disruptions are inevitable.

Cyberattacks, cloud outages, infrastructure failures, pandemics, natural disasters, supply chain interruptions and human error can significantly affect business operations.

Business Continuity Management (BCM) ensures that the organization continues delivering critical products and services despite adverse conditions.

The EVOXA Business Continuity Platform integrates technology resilience, operational procedures, crisis governance, Disaster Recovery (DR), Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), Security Operations and Enterprise Risk Management into a unified resilience strategy.

Business continuity becomes a strategic organizational capability rather than an emergency response process.

# Key Takeaways

- Business Continuity ensures that essential business capabilities continue operating despite technology failures, cyber incidents or organizational disruptions.
- Business Impact Analysis, continuity planning and crisis management provide structured governance for organizational resilience.
- AI-assisted risk analysis, recovery prioritization and executive decision support improve organizational preparedness and recovery effectiveness.
- This Business Continuity framework complements Disaster Recovery by focusing on maintaining business operations rather than only restoring technology, creating a comprehensive resilience strategy across the EVOXA ecosystem.
