# 25 — Cost Management

> *The Cost Management chapter defines the enterprise framework for measuring, allocating, optimizing and governing operational technology costs across the EVOXA ecosystem. It establishes financial visibility, cost accountability, cloud cost optimization, resource efficiency and AI-assisted financial operations to maximize business value while minimizing operational expenditure.*

---

# Executive Summary

Modern enterprise platforms operate across multiple cloud providers, Kubernetes clusters, SaaS platforms, AI services, databases and distributed infrastructure.

Without disciplined cost management, organizations experience uncontrolled cloud spending, resource waste, poor budgeting and limited financial visibility.

The EVOXA Cost Management Platform provides centralized governance for infrastructure costs, cloud consumption, AI workloads, software licensing and operational expenses through continuous monitoring, forecasting and optimization.

Cost management becomes a continuous engineering discipline integrated into daily operations.
