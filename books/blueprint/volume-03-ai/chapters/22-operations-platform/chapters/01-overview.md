# 01 — Overview

> *The Operations Platform provides the enterprise operating model responsible for ensuring that every technology service across the EVOXA ecosystem is reliable, observable, scalable, automated and continuously improving. It defines how enterprise platforms are operated, monitored, maintained and optimized throughout their entire lifecycle.*

---

# Executive Summary

Enterprise operations are no longer limited to maintaining infrastructure.

Modern digital organizations operate thousands of cloud resources, microservices, APIs, AI workloads, databases, Kubernetes clusters and business applications that require continuous monitoring, automation and operational governance.

The EVOXA Operations Platform establishes a unified operational framework that combines IT Service Management (ITSM), Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), Platform Engineering, Observability, Operational Analytics and Artificial Intelligence for IT Operations (AIOps).

This platform enables highly available digital services while minimizing operational risk, reducing manual work and improving customer experience.
