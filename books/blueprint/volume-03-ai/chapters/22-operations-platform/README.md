# Overview

The **Operations Platform** defines the enterprise operational architecture responsible for delivering reliable, scalable, observable and continuously improving digital services across the EVOXA ecosystem.

This volume establishes the enterprise operating model covering:

- IT Operations
- Platform Operations
- Service Management
- Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)
- Platform Engineering
- Operational Automation
- Monitoring
- Observability
- Incident Management
- Change Management
- Operational Governance
- AI Operations (AIOps)
- Continuous Improvement

The Operations Platform ensures that enterprise systems remain highly available, resilient, cost-efficient and aligned with business objectives.
