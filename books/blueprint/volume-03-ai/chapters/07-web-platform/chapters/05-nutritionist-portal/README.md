# Nutritionist Portal

The **Nutritionist Portal** is the professional workspace for nutritionists, dietitians and clinical nutrition specialists within the EVOXA Platform.

It provides an intelligent environment for creating personalized nutrition plans, managing dietary assessments, monitoring adherence, collaborating with coaches and healthcare professionals, and leveraging AI-powered nutritional recommendations.

The Nutritionist Portal integrates seamlessly with the Human Digital Twin, AI Platform and the broader EVOXA ecosystem to deliver evidence-based nutritional care.

---

## Objectives

The Nutritionist Portal enables professionals to:

- Manage nutrition clients.
- Perform nutritional assessments.
- Create personalized meal plans.
- Monitor nutritional adherence.
- Manage supplements.
- Track anthropometric measurements.
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams.
- Receive AI-assisted recommendations.
- Analyze nutritional progress.
- Support preventive and clinical nutrition.
