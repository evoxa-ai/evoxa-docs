# Enterprise Portal

> Enterprise administration platform for organizations using the EVOXA ecosystem.

---

## Overview

The Enterprise Portal is the corporate management platform of EVOXA.

It enables organizations to manage employees, wellness programs, corporate health initiatives, licenses, analytics, AI services and Human Digital Twin insights through a unified web experience.

The portal is designed for medium and large organizations operating across multiple business units, regions and countries.

---

## Objectives

The Enterprise Portal enables organizations to:

- Manage organizational structures.
- Administer employees.
- Configure wellness programs.
- Monitor corporate health.
- Manage subscriptions.
- Analyze organizational KPIs.
- Integrate with enterprise systems.
- Leverage AI-powered insights.
