# Chapter 08 — Screen Catalog

> *The Screen Catalog chapter defines the complete inventory of user interfaces that compose the Enterprise Portal, including dashboards, management modules, administrative screens, AI interfaces and configuration pages.*

---

# Executive Summary

The Enterprise Portal consists of a comprehensive collection of enterprise-grade interfaces designed for operational management, executive decision making and organizational governance.

Screens are organized by business domain to improve discoverability, maintainability and scalability.

Each screen has a clearly defined purpose, ownership and interaction model.

---

# Objectives

The Screen Catalog shall:

- Document every screen.
- Standardize naming.
- Organize screens by domain.
- Support UX consistency.
- Enable traceability.
- Simplify development.
- Facilitate testing.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This chapter is complete when:

- Every enterprise screen is identified and categorized.
- Screen purposes and primary personas are documented.
- Screen metadata is standardized.
- Navigation hierarchy and classifications are defined.
- Relationships with APIs, workflows and events are traceable.
- Visual artifacts are complete and aligned with the Information Architecture.

---

# Key Takeaways

- The Enterprise Portal organizes its user interfaces into business-oriented domains that reflect the operational structure of enterprise organizations.
- A standardized screen catalog ensures consistency across dashboards, management modules, analytics workspaces and administrative functions.
- Each screen is linked to personas, permissions, APIs, workflows and events, providing complete traceability throughout the platform.
- The catalog establishes the foundation for UI design, frontend development, testing and long-term product evolution.
