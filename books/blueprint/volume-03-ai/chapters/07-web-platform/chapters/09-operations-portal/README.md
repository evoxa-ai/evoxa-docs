# Overview

The Operations Portal is the operational command center of the EVOXA AI Platform.

It provides real-time monitoring, incident response, operational governance, reliability management, service health visualization, deployment supervision and AI Operations (AIOps) capabilities.

Unlike the AI Console, which focuses on designing and managing AI assets, the Operations Portal is responsible for operating the platform in production.

---

# Objectives

The Operations Portal enables organizations to:

- Monitor platform health.
- Supervise AI services.
- Manage incidents.
- Observe workflows.
- Monitor prompts and models.
- Execute operational runbooks.
- Analyze costs.
- Ensure SLA/SLO compliance.
- Support Site Reliability Engineering.
- Enable continuous operational improvement.
