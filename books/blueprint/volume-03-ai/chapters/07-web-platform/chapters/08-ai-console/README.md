## Overview

The AI Console is the centralized administration platform for all Artificial Intelligence capabilities within the EVOXA ecosystem.

It enables administrators, AI engineers, data scientists and platform operators to configure, monitor, govern and optimize AI services from a unified interface.

The AI Console is designed as an AI Operating Center rather than a traditional chatbot interface.

---

## Objectives

The AI Console provides centralized management for:

- AI Models
- Model Providers
- AI Gateway
- Prompt Studio
- Prompt Registry
- Prompt Evaluation
- AI Agents
- Multi-Agent Systems
- AI Workflows
- MCP Servers
- MCP Clients
- RAG Pipelines
- Vector Databases
- Knowledge Bases
- AI Security
- Guardrails
- AI Governance
- AI Monitoring
- AI Analytics
- AI Marketplace
- AI Cost Management
