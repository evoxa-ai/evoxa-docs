# EVOXA Admin Portal

> Enterprise-grade administration portal for managing the entire EVOXA ecosystem.

---

## Overview

The Admin Portal is the central administration console of the EVOXA platform.

It provides platform administrators with a unified interface to manage tenants, organizations, users, subscriptions, AI services, security, integrations, observability and global platform configuration.

Unlike customer-facing portals, the Admin Portal focuses on platform governance, operational control and enterprise administration.

---

## Purpose

The portal enables administrators to:

- Manage the entire EVOXA platform.
- Configure global platform settings.
- Administer tenants and organizations.
- Control authentication and authorization.
- Manage subscriptions and licensing.
- Monitor platform health.
- Operate AI services.
- Configure integrations.
- Audit platform activity.
- Maintain operational governance.
