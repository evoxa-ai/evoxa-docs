# Chapter 17 — Business Rules

> *The Business Rules chapter defines the policies, validations and operational constraints that govern every workflow, feature and AI-assisted interaction within the Coach Portal.*

---

# Executive Summary

Business Rules define the operational behavior of the Coach Portal.

They ensure that workflows are executed consistently, safely and in compliance with organizational policies, regulatory requirements and professional standards.

Business Rules are technology-independent and serve as the single source of truth for functional behavior.

---

# Objectives

The Business Rules shall:

- Standardize business logic.
- Prevent inconsistent behavior.
- Support workflow validation.
- Enable AI governance.
- Ensure compliance.
- Improve traceability.
- Support auditing.

---

# Business Rule Philosophy

Every business decision must be governed by an explicit rule.

Business Rules shall:

- Be uniquely identified.
- Be version controlled.
- Be testable.
- Be traceable.
- Be independent of implementation.

# Validation Rules

## BR-0001

A client profile cannot be activated until all mandatory fields have been completed.

---

## BR-0002

Email addresses must be unique within the organization.

---

## BR-0003

A client must accept the privacy policy before participating in any coaching program.

---

## BR-0004

Mandatory assessments must be completed before assigning the first training program.

---

# Training Rules

## BR-0100

Only active clients may receive new training programs.

---

## BR-0101

Every published training program must have an assigned coach.

---

## BR-0102

Training plans require at least one measurable objective.

---

## BR-0103

Archived programs cannot be modified.

---

# Nutrition Rules

## BR-0200

Only licensed nutrition professionals may publish nutrition plans.

---

## BR-0201

Nutrition plans must include a review schedule.

---

## BR-0202

Dietary recommendations generated by AI require professional approval before publication.

---

# Assessment Rules

## BR-0300

Initial assessments are mandatory for every new client.

---

## BR-0301

Assessment results become read-only after approval.

---

## BR-0302

Assessment history cannot be deleted.

---

# Scheduling Rules

## BR-0400

Appointments cannot overlap for the same professional.

---

## BR-0401

Cancelled appointments must retain audit history.

---

## BR-0402

Clients receive reminders according to organization settings.

---

# Messaging Rules

## BR-0500

Messages are retained according to the organization's retention policy.

---

## BR-0501

Only participants may access conversation history.

---

## BR-0502

Deleted messages remain available in the audit log when required by policy.

---

# AI Governance Rules

## BR-0600

AI recommendations are advisory.

Professionals retain final responsibility.

---

## BR-0601

Every AI recommendation shall include an explanation.

---

## BR-0602

Confidence scores shall be displayed whenever AI produces recommendations.

---

## BR-0603

AI cannot perform irreversible actions without explicit user confirmation.

---

## BR-0604

Professional feedback shall be recorded to continuously improve AI performance.

---

# Human Digital Twin Rules

## BR-0700

Every active client owns exactly one Human Digital Twin.

---

## BR-0701

Human Digital Twins are automatically updated after approved assessments.

---

## BR-0702

Only authorized services may modify Digital Twin data.

---

# Authorization Rules

## BR-0800

Users may only access organizations they belong to.

---

## BR-0801

Permissions are evaluated before every protected operation.

---

## BR-0802

Administrative operations require elevated privileges.

---

## BR-0803

Sensitive client information follows the principle of least privilege.

---

# Audit Rules

## BR-0900

Critical operations must generate immutable audit events.

---

## BR-0901

Audit logs cannot be modified by end users.

---

## BR-0902

All workflow approvals are auditable.

---

# Billing Rules

## BR-1000

Invoices cannot be modified after issuance.

---

## BR-1001

Expired subscriptions automatically restrict premium capabilities.

---

## BR-1002

Payment failures generate notification events.

---

# Compliance Rules

The Coach Portal supports compliance with:

- GDPR
- HIPAA (deployment dependent)
- ISO 27001
- SOC 2
- Local health regulations

Compliance rules are configurable per deployment.
