# Chapter 1 — Front Matter

> *Architecture defines what the platform is. Platform Design defines what users experience.*

---

# Purpose of this Volume

Volume III defines the complete functional design of the EVOXA Platform.

While previous volumes describe the vision, philosophy, and architectural foundations, this volume specifies how every application, platform, module, workflow, interface, and interaction shall be designed before implementation begins.

It is the authoritative design reference for the EVOXA ecosystem.

# Design Philosophy

The EVOXA Platform follows a Human-Centered Design philosophy.

Every design decision shall support one or more of the following objectives:

- Improve user understanding.
- Reduce cognitive load.
- Encourage healthy behaviors.
- Increase long-term adherence.
- Support scientific decision-making.
- Enable responsible AI collaboration.
- Provide accessible and inclusive experiences.

Technology exists to support people.

# Traceability

Every design decision shall be traceable to one or more architectural sources.

Typical traceability path:

```text

        │
```
