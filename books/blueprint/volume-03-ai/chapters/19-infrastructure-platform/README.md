# Volume 19 — Infrastructure Platform

> Enterprise Blueprint Documentation

---

## Overview

The **Infrastructure Platform** defines the complete cloud-native infrastructure that powers the EVOXA Enterprise Platform.

It establishes the architecture, operational standards, infrastructure automation, networking, compute, storage, security, Kubernetes ecosystem, multi-cloud strategy, observability and operational governance required to operate EVOXA at enterprise scale.

Infrastructure is treated as a product, fully automated, observable and continuously evolving.
