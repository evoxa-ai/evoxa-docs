# Chapter 01 — Overview

> *The Marketplace is the commercial, collaborative and extensible ecosystem of the EVOXA Platform. It enables organizations, partners, developers and AI creators to discover, distribute, monetize and govern reusable digital assets that accelerate enterprise automation and Artificial Intelligence adoption.*

---

# Executive Summary

The EVOXA Marketplace is significantly more than an application store.

It is the central ecosystem through which every reusable enterprise capability can be published, shared, installed, licensed, updated and monetized.

The Marketplace connects:

- Enterprise customers
- Independent software vendors
- AI developers
- System integrators
- Consulting partners
- Internal development teams
- Open-source communities

Through a unified governance and distribution platform.

Unlike traditional marketplaces that distribute only applications, the EVOXA Marketplace distributes every reusable enterprise artifact required to build intelligent organizations.

---

# Vision

> **Create the world's most comprehensive AI-Native Enterprise Marketplace, where workflows, agents, knowledge, integrations and enterprise intelligence become reusable digital assets.**
