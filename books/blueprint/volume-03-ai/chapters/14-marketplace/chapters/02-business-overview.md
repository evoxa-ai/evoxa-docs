# Chapter 02 — Business Overview

> *The Business Overview defines the strategic business model of the EVOXA Marketplace. It explains how the Marketplace creates value for enterprises, developers, AI creators, technology partners and service providers while establishing a sustainable commercial ecosystem around reusable enterprise assets.*

---

# Executive Summary

The EVOXA Marketplace is the commercial and collaborative foundation of the EVOXA ecosystem.

Its purpose is not merely to distribute software components but to create a global enterprise ecosystem where innovation becomes reusable, governed and monetizable.

Every workflow, AI agent, connector, dashboard, prompt or enterprise accelerator published to the Marketplace becomes a reusable business asset that can be consumed across thousands of organizations.

The Marketplace transforms enterprise software from isolated implementations into a continuously growing digital economy.

---

# Business Vision

> **Create the largest enterprise ecosystem for AI-powered automation, enabling organizations to discover, share, commercialize and continuously improve reusable business capabilities.**

# Key Takeaways

- The EVOXA Marketplace is a multi-sided business platform that connects enterprises, developers, AI creators and technology partners through a governed ecosystem of reusable digital assets.
