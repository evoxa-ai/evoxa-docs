## Overview

The Marketplace is the commercial and collaborative ecosystem of the EVOXA Platform.

It provides a centralized environment where organizations, partners, developers and AI creators can discover, publish, install, monetize and manage reusable digital assets that extend every capability of EVOXA.

Unlike a traditional plugin repository, the Marketplace is designed as an AI-Native Enterprise Marketplace capable of distributing workflows, AI agents, connectors, prompts, dashboards, templates, datasets, compliance packages and complete enterprise solutions.

The Marketplace serves as the primary distribution channel for innovation across the EVOXA ecosystem.

---

## Objectives

The Marketplace enables organizations to:

- Discover enterprise assets
- Install reusable solutions
- Publish internal assets
- Monetize commercial assets
- Share AI capabilities
- Distribute workflow templates
- Deliver connectors
- Manage versions
- Control licensing
- Govern marketplace content
