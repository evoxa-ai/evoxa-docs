# Chapter 02 — Business Overview

> *The Business Overview chapter defines the strategic vision, market positioning, business value, economic impact and adoption strategy of the EVOXA Enterprise Agent Platform. It explains why organizations require an enterprise agent platform, how intelligent agents transform knowledge work, and how EVOXA enables the transition from traditional software to an AI-native enterprise.*

---

# Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence is redefining enterprise software.

The next generation of digital platforms will not be driven solely by applications or dashboards, but by **intelligent autonomous agents** capable of understanding business objectives, collaborating with humans, coordinating with other agents and executing enterprise workflows.

The Enterprise Agent Platform enables organizations to evolve from:

```text
Software

↓

Automation

↓

Intelligent Automation

↓

Digital Workforce

↓

Autonomous Enterprise
```

Rather than replacing existing enterprise systems, AI agents become the intelligent operational layer that connects people, knowledge, applications and business processes.

---

# Business Vision

The vision of the Enterprise Agent Platform is to become the **Enterprise Operating System for Artificial Intelligence**.

Every employee should have access to specialized AI agents capable of assisting, automating and optimizing their daily work.

Every department should operate with a team composed of both humans and AI agents.

---

# Business Mission

Enable organizations to build, govern and scale enterprise AI agents safely, securely and efficiently while maximizing productivity, innovation and business value.
