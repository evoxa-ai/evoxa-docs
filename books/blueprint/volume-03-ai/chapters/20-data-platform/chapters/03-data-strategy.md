# 03 — Data Strategy

> *The Data Strategy chapter defines the long-term vision, strategic objectives, governance principles and transformation roadmap that guide the evolution of the EVOXA Enterprise Data Platform. It establishes how data becomes a strategic enterprise asset supporting analytics, artificial intelligence, operational excellence and digital innovation.*

---

# Executive Summary

Data Strategy defines how the organization acquires, governs, manages, protects and monetizes information.

Rather than treating data as a by-product of applications, EVOXA considers data to be an enterprise product with measurable business value.

The Data Strategy aligns technology, governance and business objectives into a unified roadmap that enables:

- Trusted enterprise data
- AI readiness
- Operational intelligence
- Self-service analytics
- Data democratization
- Regulatory compliance
- Continuous innovation

---

# Strategic Vision

The platform follows one guiding principle:

> **Every Enterprise Decision Is Powered by Trusted, Governed and Intelligent Data.**
