# 01 — Overview

> *The Overview chapter introduces the EVOXA Enterprise Data Platform, defining its mission, strategic objectives, architectural vision and foundational principles. It establishes the Data Platform as the central enterprise capability responsible for collecting, governing, processing, securing and delivering trusted data across the entire EVOXA ecosystem.*

---

# Executive Summary

Data has become the most valuable strategic asset of modern enterprises.

The EVOXA Data Platform provides a unified, governed and AI-ready architecture capable of supporting operational systems, analytics, machine learning and intelligent automation from a single enterprise data foundation.

Rather than maintaining isolated databases, independent analytics environments or disconnected reporting systems, the platform consolidates every stage of the data lifecycle into a single enterprise architecture.

The platform supports both operational and analytical workloads while enabling real-time decision making, self-service analytics and AI-driven business capabilities.
