# 24 — Knowledge Platform

# 01 — Overview

## 1. Introduction

The Knowledge Platform is a foundational capability of the EVOXA AI ecosystem responsible for transforming distributed organizational data, documents, information, metadata, events, structured records and domain knowledge into governed, discoverable, reusable and AI-accessible knowledge.

The Knowledge Platform provides the knowledge foundation required by:

- AI Services
- AI Assistants
- AI Agents
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Decision Intelligence
- Analytics
- Search
- Automation
- Customer Experience
- Operational Intelligence
- Enterprise Applications

The platform establishes a common architecture through which knowledge can be:

```text
Ingested
    ↓
Processed
    ↓
Normalized
    ↓
Enriched
    ↓
Indexed
    ↓
Governed
    ↓
Retrieved
    ↓
Consumed
    ↓
Evaluated
    ↓
Continuously Improved

The Knowledge Platform therefore represents more than a document repository.

It is an enterprise capability for managing the lifecycle of knowledge required by intelligent systems.

2. Purpose

The purpose of the Knowledge Platform is to provide EVOXA with a unified foundation for managing and delivering trusted knowledge to AI Services and enterprise applications.

The platform should enable:

Knowledge ingestion.
Knowledge discovery.
Knowledge classification.
Knowledge normalization.
Knowledge enrichment.
Knowledge indexing.
Knowledge retrieval.
Knowledge governance.
Knowledge versioning.
Knowledge lineage.
Knowledge access control.
Knowledge quality management.
Knowledge lifecycle management.
Knowledge observability.
Knowledge analytics.
AI-ready knowledge delivery.
3. Strategic Objective

The strategic objective of the Knowledge Platform is:

Transform enterprise information into trusted, governed and reusable knowledge that can be safely consumed by AI Services, applications, users and automated systems.

The platform must ensure that AI systems do not operate only on models.

They must operate on:

    +
