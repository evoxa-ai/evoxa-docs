# 24 — Knowledge Platform

# 21 — Knowledge Productization

## 1. Introduction

Knowledge Productization defines the architecture, business model, service model, packaging strategy, commercial mechanisms and product lifecycle required to transform the capabilities of the EVOXA Knowledge Platform into reusable, consumable and commercially valuable products and services.

A Knowledge Platform should not remain only an internal technical capability.

Its purpose is to transform knowledge into reusable value.

The fundamental transformation is:

```text
Raw Data
   ↓
Information
   ↓
Knowledge
   ↓
Knowledge Services
   ↓
Knowledge Products
   ↓
Business Value

Knowledge Productization establishes the mechanisms required to make this transformation repeatable.

The fundamental principle is:

Knowledge becomes a product when it can be consistently delivered, consumed, measured, governed, supported and monetized.

2. Purpose

The purpose of Knowledge Productization is to establish the mechanisms required to:

Identify productizable knowledge capabilities.
Transform internal capabilities into services.
Define knowledge products.
Package knowledge capabilities.
Define product boundaries.
Define product interfaces.
Define consumption models.
Define customer value.
Define pricing models.
Define usage models.
Define service tiers.
Define product lifecycle.
Define product ownership.
Define product governance.
Define product quality.
Define product SLAs.
Define product economics.
Enable commercialization.
Enable ecosystem consumption.
Enable partner integration.
Enable future knowledge marketplaces.
3. Strategic Objective

The strategic objective is:

Transform EVOXA Knowledge Platform capabilities into reusable, measurable and commercially viable Knowledge Products that generate sustainable business value.

4. Productization Principle

The fundamental principle is:

Every productized knowledge capability must expose a clear value proposition, consumption model, service contract, quality model and economic model.
