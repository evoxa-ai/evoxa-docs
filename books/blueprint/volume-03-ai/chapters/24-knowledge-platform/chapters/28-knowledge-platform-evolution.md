# 28 — Knowledge Platform Evolution

## 1. Introduction

Knowledge Platform Evolution defines how the EVOXA Knowledge Platform evolves over time without compromising stability, governance, compatibility, security, reliability or business continuity.

A knowledge platform cannot be treated as a static technical system.

Knowledge changes.

Business requirements change.

AI capabilities change.

Data sources change.

Models change.

User expectations change.

Regulations change.

Technology changes.

Therefore:

> EVOXA must be designed to evolve continuously while preserving the integrity of the platform and the trustworthiness of the knowledge it provides.

3. Strategic Objective

The strategic objective is:

Build a Knowledge Platform capable of continuously adapting to technological, organizational and business change while maintaining architectural integrity, operational stability and long-term strategic relevance.

4. Evolution Principle
