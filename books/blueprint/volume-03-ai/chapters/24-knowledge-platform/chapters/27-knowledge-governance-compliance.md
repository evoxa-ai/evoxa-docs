# 27 — Knowledge Governance & Compliance

## 1. Introduction

Knowledge Governance & Compliance defines the framework through which EVOXA ensures that knowledge, data, intelligence, agents, automations and knowledge-driven services operate according to organizational policies, security requirements, regulatory obligations, contractual commitments and business controls.

Knowledge Governance establishes:

The fundamental principle is:

EVOXA Knowledge must remain governed, accountable, traceable, secure and compliant throughout its entire lifecycle.

Define ownership of knowledge.
Establish accountability.
Define policies.

Support audits.
Measure governance effectiveness.
3. Strategic Objective

The strategic objective is:

Establish EVOXA as a governed knowledge platform where every significant knowledge asset, decision, action and automated process has defined ownership, policy, accountability, provenance and compliance controls.

4. Governance Principle

The fundamental governance principle is:

Every material knowledge capability must have an owner, a purpose, defined access rules, applicable policies, measurable controls and an auditable lifecycle.
