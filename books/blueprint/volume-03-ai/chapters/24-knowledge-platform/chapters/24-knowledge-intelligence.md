## 1. Introduction

Knowledge Intelligence defines the capabilities through which EVOXA transforms governed knowledge into higher-order understanding, insights, relationships, predictions, recommendations and decision support.

Knowledge Intelligence therefore represents the layer between knowledge availability and intelligent decision-making.

The fundamental principle is:

Knowledge Intelligence transforms governed knowledge into contextual understanding and actionable intelligence while preserving evidence, provenance, uncertainty and governance.

2. Purpose

The purpose of Knowledge Intelligence is to establish the capabilities required to:

Transform knowledge into insights.
Detect relationships.
Identify patterns.
Detect anomalies.
Generate hypotheses.
Analyze trends.
Predict future states.
Recommend actions.
Support decisions.
Connect knowledge across domains.
Combine structured and unstructured knowledge.
Combine historical and real-time knowledge.
Support AI reasoning.
Support human reasoning.
Support agent reasoning.
Preserve evidence.
Preserve provenance.
Quantify uncertainty.
Detect knowledge gaps.
Detect contradictions.
Measure intelligence quality.
Enable continuous intelligence improvement.
3. Strategic Objective

The strategic objective is:

Build a Knowledge Intelligence layer that enables EVOXA to continuously transform governed knowledge into reliable, contextual, explainable and actionable intelligence.

4. Knowledge Intelligence Principle

The fundamental principle is:

Intelligence is not the generation of more information; it is the ability to derive meaningful understanding and actionable decisions from governed knowledge.
