# 02 — Business Overview

> *The Business Overview defines the strategic business context of the EVOXA Identity Platform. It explains the organizational challenges addressed by Enterprise Identity and Access Management (IAM), the business value delivered by centralized identity services, the regulatory landscape and the strategic role of identity as the foundation of every digital enterprise.*

---

# Executive Summary

Identity has become one of the most valuable enterprise assets.

Every employee...

Every customer...

Every API...

Every AI Agent...

Every application...

Every device...

Every workflow...

Every automated service...

...must possess a verifiable identity before interacting with enterprise resources.

Without a centralized identity platform organizations experience:

- Security gaps
- Identity silos
- Inconsistent authentication
- Excessive privileges
- Compliance failures
- Poor user experience
- High operational costs

The EVOXA Identity Platform solves these challenges through a unified enterprise identity architecture.

---

- The EVOXA Identity Platform centralizes identity management, eliminates fragmented authentication systems and enables secure access across humans, machines and AI agents.
- Zero Trust, identity governance and automation reduce operational costs while strengthening security and regulatory compliance.
- The platform provides the trusted digital identity foundation required for enterprise modernization, AI adoption and long-term business growth.
