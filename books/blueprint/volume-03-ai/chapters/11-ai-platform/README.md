# Enterprise Analytics Platform

> **Enterprise Analytics Platform** defines the complete analytics, business intelligence, reporting, semantic modeling and AI-driven insights platform of EVOXA.

---

## Purpose

The Enterprise Analytics Platform transforms operational and analytical data into actionable business intelligence.

It provides:

- Executive Dashboards
- Operational Dashboards
- KPI Management
- Semantic Metrics
- Self-Service Analytics
- Embedded Analytics
- AI Insights
- Predictive Analytics
- Natural Language Analytics
- Enterprise Reporting

This chapter establishes the architecture that enables organizations to understand, monitor and optimize every aspect of their business.

---
