# 05 — User Journeys

> *The User Journeys chapter defines how every persona interacts with the EVOXA Analytics Platform to accomplish analytical, operational and strategic objectives. These journeys describe complete end-to-end workflows from authentication through data exploration, AI-assisted analysis, collaboration and business decision-making.*

---

# Executive Summary

The Analytics Platform is built around **decision workflows**, not reports.

Users do not simply view dashboards.

They:

- Explore data
- Monitor KPIs
- Investigate anomalies
- Ask AI questions
- Build reports
- Share insights
- Trigger business actions

Every journey is optimized to reduce the time between **data** and **decision**.

---

# Journey Vision

The platform follows one principle:

> **From Question to Decision in the Fewest Possible Steps.**

# Key Takeaways

- The EVOXA Analytics Platform is designed around complete decision-making journeys rather than isolated dashboards or reports.
- AI is embedded throughout every workflow, accelerating data exploration, analysis, explanation and business decision-making.
- Personalized journeys ensure executives, analysts, managers, developers and administrators each receive an optimized analytical experience.
- This user journey architecture minimizes the distance between enterprise data and actionable business outcomes.
