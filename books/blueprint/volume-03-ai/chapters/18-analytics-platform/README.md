# Volume 18 — Analytics Platform

> The Analytics Platform is the enterprise intelligence layer of EVOXA. It transforms operational data into actionable insights through Business Intelligence, AI-powered analytics, real-time dashboards, predictive models and enterprise reporting.

---

# Purpose

The Analytics Platform centralizes analytical capabilities across every EVOXA platform and business domain.

It provides:

- Enterprise Business Intelligence
- Executive Dashboards
- Operational Analytics
- Real-Time Analytics
- AI-Powered Insights
- Predictive Analytics
- Embedded Analytics
- Self-Service Analytics
- KPI Management
- Enterprise Reporting

---

# Vision

> **Every Decision Should Be Data-Driven.**

Analytics is treated as a core platform capability rather than an isolated reporting tool.
