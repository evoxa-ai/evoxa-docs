# Roadmap

# BP-0002 — EVOXA Blueprint

## Volume II — Reference Architecture

---

## Purpose

This roadmap defines the planned evolution of the EVOXA Reference Architecture.

It serves as the long-term architectural planning document for the platform and provides guidance for future architectural decisions, engineering initiatives, and platform evolution.

Unlike the CHANGELOG, which records completed work, the Roadmap describes planned capabilities and architectural milestones.

---

# Vision

The EVOXA architecture is designed to evolve continuously while preserving stability, modularity, and maintainability.

The roadmap is organized into architectural releases rather than software releases.

Each release represents a significant increase in architectural maturity.

# Success Criteria

The roadmap is considered successful when:

- Every architectural milestone is documented.
- All domains remain aligned with the Blueprint.
- Engineering Standards remain synchronized.
- Implementation follows architectural guidance.
- AI capabilities remain consistent with Human-Centered Intelligence.
