For readers approaching EVOXA for the first time, the recommended reading order is:

2. Blueprint Volume II — Reference Architecture
3. Engineering Standards (EES)
4. AI Engineering Standards
5. Engineering Specifications (ESP)
6. Implementation Stories (IS)

This sequence progresses from strategic concepts to architecture, engineering practices, detailed specifications, and implementation.
