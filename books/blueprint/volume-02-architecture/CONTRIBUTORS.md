# Purpose

This document defines the official contribution model for **Blueprint Volume II — Reference Architecture**.

Rather than listing names only, it establishes the governance structure, contributor roles, responsibilities, and review process required to maintain the quality and consistency of the EVOXA documentation ecosystem.

Every contribution shall follow the engineering, editorial, and architectural standards established by EVOXA.

# Contribution Rules

All contributors shall:

- Follow the official terminology.
- Respect architectural principles.
- Maintain traceability.
- Update cross references.
- Update changelog entries.
- Preserve documentation quality.

Contributors shall not:

- Introduce conflicting architectural concepts.
- Duplicate terminology.
- Modify normative principles without approval.
- Bypass review processes.

---

# AI Contributors

AI assistants are recognized as engineering collaborators.

Examples include:

- Claude
- ChatGPT
- Cursor
- GitHub Copilot

AI contributions shall always be reviewed by a human before publication.

AI assistants:

- Generate drafts.
- Propose improvements.
- Assist implementation.
- Suggest documentation.
- Never become the final authority.

Human oversight is mandatory.
